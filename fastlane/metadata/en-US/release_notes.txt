FOOD PICTURES

In German there is a proverb that says "Das Auge isst mit" which means that you not only consume food with your mouth but also with your eyes. This is why you can now photograph your food and add it to the canteen menu!

Next to each meal there is a photo gallery of pictures of the food as well as a button with which you can photogeaph your food within seconds. If a canteen serves the same meal again in the future, the photo will be reused.

Also, we have now merged the UZH and ETH canteens in the center of Zurich so that you can see all options at once.
